Our neighborhood story

How we began

North Star began with a simple idea: a bakery should feel like part of the neighborhood. We make everyday breads and treats in small batches, leaving room to greet familiar faces and welcome new ones.

What goes into our baking

We choose quality ingredients, look for seasonal flavors, and favor nearby suppliers when practical. We make thoughtful portions and plan our baking around what our neighbors enjoy.

Meet the team

Our bakers start early to prepare the day's loaves and pastries. Our counter team helps customers choose a treat, plan a pickup, or find something to bring to a gathering. Every person helps make the bakery welcoming.
